# BIT Intelligence System Upgrade Roadmap

## Decision

Build **one richer daily product with two same-date revisions**, not a second Hong Kong website and not a twenty-five-headline feed. The research process expands to twenty U.S./global slots and five Hong Kong-close lenses; the reader sees only the few evidence-backed analytical chains that materially change the thesis.

This sequence protects the current working product while lifting its explanatory quality. The 1 September release and its historical pages remain unchanged until an approved module is implemented and validated.

## Phase 1 — Data and revision foundation

Add a dated AM/PM revision envelope—`edition`, `cutoff_hkt`, `revision_timestamp`, `revision_history`—to the report data contract. Preserve the current archive date model; a PM close update is a revision of the same date, not a second daily archive record. Extend the thesis record with market regime/confidence, pricing question and prior-watch statuses.

**Exit criteria:** data validators reject an AM/PM timestamp conflict, intraday-as-close wording, a missing prior revision, or a PM revision that lacks a completed-HK evidence boundary.

## Phase 2 — Morning intelligence modules

Prototype three small reusable modules on one future report date: Market Regime, Thesis Ledger and Driver/Transmission Chain. Use the existing six-section architecture and progressive disclosure. Add an Expectation Gap card only for earnings/events with a reliable expectation, actual/guidance and price-reaction record; do not fabricate consensus or implied moves.

**Exit criteria:** the first screen answers regime, price driver and next test within 60 seconds; the expanded layer explains the evidence chain without increasing headline count; TW/CN/EN, Education/Analysis, PDF and mobile checks pass.

## Phase 3 — Hong Kong close intelligence

After 17:00 HKT, update the same date using a Flow Confirmation card and seven analytical lenses: index/breadth; flow; liquidity; China policy/macro; sector leadership; company/HKEX disclosures; and Hong Kong-to-U.S. read-through. Use official HKEX, Hang Seng Indexes, HKMA, HKEXnews, PBOC/NBS/SAFE/CSRC/State Council sources first. State `UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED` when turnover, Southbound, positioning or funding data is missing.

**Exit criteria:** the PM layer explains who drove the index, whether participation/flow confirmed it, and whether there is an evidenced U.S. read-through. It never treats a morning snapshot as a close.

## Phase 4 — Weekly and cross-market boards

Transform **This Week** into a Weekly Thesis Board: event date/time, expectation, what appears priced, assets affected, positive/negative condition, what changes the thesis and post-event status. Transform **Cross-market** into an evidence loop: U.S. close → Asia/HK response → HK close → next U.S. implication, with driver, mechanism, observed evidence and status.

**Exit criteria:** each correlation has an articulated mechanism; unconfirmed links are marked partial/unresolved; weekly review can compare Monday thesis with Friday evidence.

## Approval boundary

Implement this roadmap one module at a time through `[ideation]` → local prototype → `[plan]` → explicit approval → supervised real-use test. Do not redesign the BIT shell, replace the six sections, remove heatmap controls, alter old reports or create a publishing schedule until an AM/PM supervised cycle has passed all public-release checks.
