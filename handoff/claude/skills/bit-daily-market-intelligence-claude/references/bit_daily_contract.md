# BIT Daily Contract

## Editorial contract

Use the actual `Asia/Hong_Kong` clock. Clearly separate prior completed U.S. session, prior completed Hong Kong session, timestamped intraday Hong Kong observation and scheduled future catalysts. Read user-provided material first; verify it independently. Use Tier 1 primary sources for facts, Tier 2 for reputable market context and Tier 3 only for leads/style unless confirmed.

Audit yesterday’s thesis before drafting. Mark every watch item confirmed, partially confirmed, invalidated or unresolved. Keep the daily causal chain narrow: one to three drivers, not a headline list. Explain why the completed session moved and which conditions can confirm or weaken that interpretation next.

## Reader contract

Maintain complete Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and English across Overview, U.S., Hong Kong, Cross-market, This Week and Sources. Education teaches interpretation; Analysis expresses thesis, confirmation and invalidation conditions. Use neutral internal research language and the non-advice disclosure.

## Product contract

Use the approved BIT white/black/blue shell; do not return to personal bylines, ivory/brass styling or dark cyber styling. Keep the fixed latest root, visible date archive, query-based history, mobile-visible language/PDF controls and browser-native six-section PDF route.

In the U.S. page and full PDF: completed session → macro/market drivers → Stocks / Industry Chain fact–meaning–next-validation cards → dark Sector Heatmap. Retain 1D/5D/20D controls, green/grey/orange-red semantics and the 11-sector grid. Do not restore retired SVG export or removed summary cards.

For Hong Kong: completed close → timestamped intraday observation if real → post-close turnover/Stock Connect check → next validation. Never call intraday data a close.

## Publication contract

Preserve historical reports and inspect the actual current router/hub before editing. Test applicable data validation, `pnpm check`, `pnpm build`, local desktop, 390px mobile, export PDF, languages, reading modes, heatmap controls, archive history and browser console. Commit only to the company repository; deploy only after all gates pass. Verify a cache-busted public root before stating that a release is live.
